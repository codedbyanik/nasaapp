Slope is computed at the source's 20 m spacing with a longitude-spacing correction. A ~100 m routing cell is excluded if its maximum native slope reaches 20° or if data are incomplete. This is a prototype policy, not a NASA EVA limit.

Elevation residual measures difference from a Gaussian-smoothed DEM at ~40 m scale; it does not classify rocks or sand. Terrain exposure is a normalized cost, not measured risk.

Distances use mean Mars radius 3,389.5 km; source projection uses its recorded radius 3,396.19 km. One-way walk speed 3 km/h and oxygen 0.8 kg/h +30% reserve are unvalidated demo assumptions. No live weather, mineral raster, or hardware telemetry is connected.
